Ledger management

Important: Use this section only for authorized accounting corrections or transactions that have been independently verified. A ledger entry is not proof that funds were received. Never credit an account based solely on a client's claim or an unverified payment screenshot.

Credits and debits are submitted to the Supabase admin_post_ledger_entry function. The database must enforce active administrator permissions, reference uniqueness, input validation, and overdraft prevention.

Ledger management is ready when the client account list has loaded.
