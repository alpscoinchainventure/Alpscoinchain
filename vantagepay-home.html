Important information about VantagePay

VantagePay is a proposed platform currently in development; this website does not represent that VantagePay is a licensed bank, deposit-taking institution or live investment service. Real balances, deposits, withdrawals, investment execution and APY credits are not currently active or guaranteed. VantagePay is intended to operate as a standalone product. Any future APY relationship with a separate, appropriately authorized investment provider must be formally configured, independently verified and reflected in confirmed account records before returns are credited. No money should be sent based on this preview. Identity-document collection and KYC are intentionally not active in this phase.
