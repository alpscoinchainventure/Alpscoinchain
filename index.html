Frequently asked questions

Before you decide.

Important basics for anyone exploring the platform.

Are returns guaranteed?

No. Financial markets are uncertain and you may lose some or all of the capital you put at risk. No return is promised on this page.

What are the minimum amounts and fees?

These have not been published here because verified product terms must be confirmed first. Ask the team for written details before proceeding.

Do charts execute trades?

No. The market charts are informational widgets from a third party. They do not place trades or connect to your portfolio.

Does an approved deposit request mean funds arrived?

Not by itself. A request status is an administrative record and is not independent proof of an external payment or completed transfer.

How do I access my account?

Use Client sign in at the top of this page. Keep your password, private keys, and recovery phrases confidential.

How can I contact support?

Use the secure contact form below. Do not send passwords, private keys, recovery phrases, or full payment credentials.
